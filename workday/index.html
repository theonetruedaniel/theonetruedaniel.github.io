From incoming lead to customer support.

I combined sales with GTM operations and served as HubSpot superadmin. My work connected qualification, lead-specific sequences and sales ownership with the deal process and the handoff into customer-support workflows.

An overview of my work at AdScale. Customer records and internal configurations are omitted.

How the process connected

  1. Receive & enrichFacebook leads, app installs and website inquiries arrived through webhooks. Clay and Zapier helped add contact and business context.
  2. Qualify the leadWebsite fit, traffic and other business signals informed lead classification and prioritization in HubSpot.
  3. Select sequence & ownerHubSpot conditions matched the lead type to the appropriate person, email, follow-up sequence and duration.
  4. Consult & manage the dealThe Growth Potential Analysis informed the sales conversation. Deal stages, activity, ownership and next steps stayed in HubSpot.
  5. Hand off to supportAfter conversion, the customer moved into the customer-support process, with its own sequence of workflows and follow-through.

A consultative sales process, built around analysis

I redesigned the process from a traditional SaaS demo and purchase decision into a consultative Growth Potential Analysis. It brought the prospect’s campaigns, ad performance and business into the conversation before recommending a next step.

  1. Understand the business and market. Start with the prospect’s goals, business niche, market conditions and competitors.
  2. Connect customer behavior to performance. Bring order data, customer segments, average order value and repeat-purchase behavior into the campaign review. The analysis prompts called for checking personas against order data.
  3. Compare performance over time. I specified reviews across 7, 30, 90 and 180 days and the past 12 months, covering ad spend, revenue, acquisition cost and return on ad spend.
  4. Turn findings into priorities. Connect patterns and bottlenecks to specific recommendations, grouped into quick wins, longer-term opportunities and tests, with impact, effort and timing made explicit.
  5. Discuss scenarios and fit. Use the dashboard and revenue calculator to explore potential improvements, then discuss whether AdScale addressed the customer’s needs. Modeled gains were scenarios, not achieved results.

My design decisions

I developed and refined the AI prompts and dashboard requirements around a consultative conversation. I required data to be checked against the supplied sources and projections to stay grounded in current average ad spend and revenue. I also merged overlapping sections into one Insights view so the same finding would not be repeated across tabs.

From sales tool to product feature: the dashboard was later incorporated into AdScale’s product. I created the original dashboard and analysis approach as part of the sales-process redesign.

What I configured and maintained

The sequence and owner depended on the lead. Keeping that logic useful also meant maintaining the records and handoffs across the customer journey.

Lead-specific follow-up

My work: Connect qualification to conditional assignment and follow-up. Lead type and business signals influenced who received the lead and which sequence it entered.

What I checked: Leads entered the appropriate workflow and sequence; I reviewed and corrected missed enrollment.

Accurate contact records

My work: Review fragmented booking, form and app-install records before merging. Check identity rather than relying on timing alone.

What I checked: Lead Quality, Lead Status and Marketing Source remained correct after the merge.

Continuity through the handoff

My work: Maintain deal stages and next steps, and coordinate the transition from sales into customer support and its workflows, including onboarding and billing context.

What I checked: Open deals had activity and a credible next step; handoffs included the account context needed for follow-through.

Faster initial outreach

Up to 24 hours → automated outreach within 3 minutes. Connecting screening, routing and outreach reduced the initial wait in the manual process. This historical result is based on my experience and refers to automated outreach, not the time to close a deal or resolve a support issue.
