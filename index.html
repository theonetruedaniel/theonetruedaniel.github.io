The workflow I built

  1. Receive & enrich. Bring Facebook leads, app installs, and website inquiries through webhooks. Normalize contact data and add traffic and company context.
  2. Screen & route. Use website-fit analysis and business signals to grade leads, update HubSpot, and assign the appropriate owner and follow-up.
  3. Check & maintain. Merge fragmented records, preserve source attribution, check sequence enrollment, and resolve missed or incomplete lead handling.
Read the full AdScale case study

The problem

Salespeople had to research incoming leads, decide whether they fit, and choose a next step. That process could leave a lead waiting for up to a day. Bookings, forms, and app installs also created separate records for the same person.

My contribution

I connected Clay, Zapier, webhooks, and HubSpot to enrich and categorize incoming leads. Screening considered the business’s products, average order value, website quality, traffic, and other company signals. HubSpot conditions controlled ownership, email, and follow-up sequences.

I also supplied roughly 70,000–76,000 prospect contacts for AdScale. That outbound sourcing work was separate from the inbound response-time improvement.

HubSpot administration

I maintained workflows, lead-grading rules, pipeline stages, and reporting, and worked on two CRM migrations. Daily cleanup included merging duplicate or fragmented contacts and reviewing Lead Quality, Lead Status, and Marketing Source after each merge.

I checked whether leads entered the correct sequence and handled missed enrollment. I kept bookings, ownership, and next steps aligned with the record so the team could act on the information.

Result

Automated screening, routing, and outreach brought the first outreach within three minutes. My sales and operations work contributed to AdScale Plus growing annual revenue from approximately $1.5M to $3M over one year.

02

Market-research engagement · Work through a consulting partner

Nearly 30,000 prospects, organized for follow-up.

I screened an existing Clay-enriched contact export for a market-research business and delivered 28,552 potential-fit prospect companies with selected contacts, study angles, and evidence labels.

76,133source contact rows screened→28,552prospect companies delivered
Read the prospect-research case study

The problem

The team had a large contact database. They needed a usable prospect list: relevant businesses, a contact to approach, and a reason a research study might help that business.

My contribution

I screened the full source list, reconciled its rows, and identified 11,277 duplicate contact rows. I organized potential fits by study category and geography, preserved unknowns in a review queue, and supplied a Google Sheet with a CSV backup.

The 28,552-company list included a selected existing contact for each company. I kept 9,405 unresolved company groups separate so gaps in evidence would remain visible.

Targeted enrichment

A separate research batch produced 153 contacts across 144 companies, covering agencies and direct buyers. I used public evidence and Clay enrichment and labeled recorded email addresses by source. I did not add that batch to the main count because the lists may overlap.

Result and limits

The delivery gave the team a segmented prospect list and research context for follow-up. “Potential fit” describes general relevance. The count does not represent active buyers, verified email deliverability, meetings, or sales.

AdScale operating notes: CRM cleanup and follow-through

Adapted from my June 2026 handover instructions. This is a paraphrased checklist; I have omitted private customer details.

  • Review duplicate contacts. Check the person behind booking, form and app-install records before merging. Preserve Lead Quality, Lead Status and Marketing Source.
  • Check follow-up enrollment. Confirm that each lead enters the appropriate workflow and sequence; review missed enrollment and correct it.
  • Keep opportunities actionable. Tie open deals to an owner, an activity and a credible next step. Check bookings to prevent duplicate slots.
  • Prepare the handoff. Document account context for customer onboarding and billing coordination.

These notes describe my operating practices. They are not a screenshot or export from AdScale’s HubSpot account.
